I am a geospatial specialist, urban researcher, and humanitarian data management lead dedicated to turning complex spatial data into actionable insights across built environments, informal settlements, and crisis contexts. Having worked on projects in over 30 countries and collected spatial data in-situ for personal or professional projects in more than 80 countries. Currently I work at Humanitarian OpenStreetMap where I help international organizations architect resilient data pipelines, analyze urban spatial morphology, and deploy open-source GIS tools to ensure evidence-based decision-making where it matters most.
